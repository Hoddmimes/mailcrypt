mailcrypt

Encrypt text with a shared password, paste it into any mail. Everything happens in this page; nothing is sent anywhere.

At least 8 characters. Give the recipient the password some other way: SMS, Signal, phone. Never in the same mail.

Quote marks (>), line breaks and surrounding mail text are fine.

Loaded from the link.

AES-256-GCM, key from PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations), new random salt and IV per message. Runs offline: you can save this page and open it from disk.
